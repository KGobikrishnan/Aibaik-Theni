# Kural (குரல்) — Your Customer's Voice

> **Kural (குரல்)** is a smart AI-powered customer feedback and Google Review management platform designed for local businesses (boutiques, salons, clinics, cafes, retail stores, automotive workshops) to effortlessly collect authentic, high-quality Google Reviews.

Instead of customers struggling to type a long review or leaving a blank star rating, Kural captures their natural thoughts—through simple voice input or quick prompts—and instantly turns them into well-written, SEO-friendly Google Reviews ready to post in one tap.

---

## 1. How Kural Works (Customer Journey)

1. **Scan or Tap**: The customer scans a Kural QR standee at the billing counter or clicks a link sent via WhatsApp / SMS.
2. **Speak or Tap Experience**: They select their star rating and either speak a few words in Tamil, Tanglish, or English (e.g., *"Service romba nalla irunthuchu, collection super"*) or tap quick experience tags.
3. **AI Review Generation**: Kural's AI converts their rough input into a natural, polite, and keyword-rich Google Review.
4. **One-Tap Post**: The customer taps **Copy & Open Google**, which automatically copies the generated text and opens the business's Google Review page to paste and submit.

---

## 2. Core Features

- 🎙️ **Voice-to-Review (Multilingual)**: Customers don't have to type; they can speak naturally in Tamil, Tanglish, or English.
- 🚀 **SEO-Optimized Output**: Automatically weaves in relevant business keywords (e.g., *"designer boutique in Coimbatore"*, *"friendly staff"*, *"fast service"*) to help the shop rank higher on Google Maps Local 3-Pack.
- 🛡️ **Smart Feedback Filtering**: Happy customers (4–5 stars) are guided directly to Google Reviews, while lower ratings (1–3 stars) are routed to a private feedback form so the owner can resolve issues before they go public.
- 🖨️ **Custom Branded QR Standees**: Each business gets a personalized QR page and downloadable standee featuring their shop name, logo, and theme.

---

## 3. Why Local Businesses Need Kural

- **Higher Review Conversion**: Most happy customers skip leaving reviews because they don't know what to write. Kural removes that friction, turning 5x more visitors into 5-star reviewers.
- **Better Local SEO & Footfall**: More frequent, detailed reviews push the business into the Google Local 3-Pack (top 3 map results), bringing in new walk-in customers without paid ads.
- **Zero Tech Hassle for Staff**: No app installation needed for customers—works directly in any mobile browser.
